Privacy Policy — Warranty Keeper

Last updated 3 November 2026

Warranty Keeper does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account to create, no server to sign in to, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Warranty Keeper keeps the items you add — product name, purchase date, warranty length, optional price and notes — together with any receipt or product photos and PDF warranty cards you attach. All of it is written to the app's own private storage area on your device, which other apps cannot read.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. The app has no backend service.

Reading dates from your receipts

When you ask the app to find dates on an attachment, text recognition runs entirely on your device, using an on-device model bundled with the app. Your receipt image is not sent to any server, and no cloud recognition service is involved.

Network access

The app does not contact any server in normal use. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking libraries.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Notifications — to remind you before a warranty expires. Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. Declining this permission disables reminders and nothing else.
  • Biometric / device credential — only if you switch on the optional app lock. Your fingerprint or PIN is handled by Android; the app never sees or stores it.
  • Photos and files — chosen through Android's own picker when you attach a receipt. The app receives only the file you pick, and copies it into its private storage. It cannot browse your gallery.

The app does not request camera permission. Taking a photo hands off to your existing camera app.

Backups

Automatic cloud backup is switched off, so Android does not copy your warranty data or receipt images to your Google account. Direct phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new device is left enabled, because that moves data between your own two devices without passing through a server.

Settings also offers a manual Backup that produces an archive file. You choose where that file goes. If you send it to a cloud drive, email or messaging app, that data is then handled by whichever service you chose, under their privacy policy rather than this one.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties, because no data ever reaches the developer in the first place.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item removes its record and its attached files. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings › Apps › Warranty Keeper › Storage › Clear data, permanently removes everything. Because nothing is stored remotely, there is no account to close and no deletion request to submit.

Children

Warranty Keeper is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
